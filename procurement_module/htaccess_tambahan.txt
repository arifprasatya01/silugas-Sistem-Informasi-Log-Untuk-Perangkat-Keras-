Tambahkan 4 baris ini ke dalam blok RewriteRule di .htaccess,
setelah baris "RewriteRule ^berita_acara$ pages/berita_acara.php [L,QSA]":

    RewriteRule ^procurement_list$ pages/procurement_list.php [L,QSA]
    RewriteRule ^procurement_add$ pages/procurement_add.php [L,QSA]
    RewriteRule ^procurement_detail$ pages/procurement_detail.php [L,QSA]
    RewriteRule ^procurement_print$ pages/procurement_print.php [L,QSA]
